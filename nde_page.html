NAMED DRIVER EXCLUSION ENDORSEMENT

In consideration of the premium charged, the parties agree to the following amendments to the policy:

I elect to exclude coverage for the following person(s) as an Excluded Driver. I agree that no coverage is provided for any auto accident or loss arising from the use of any vehicle under the care, custody or control of any Excluded Driver, including but not limited to claims for which I or an uninsured person becomes vicariously liable. I further agree that this serves as a rejection for Part C — Uninsured Motorist Coverage while a vehicle is under the care, custody or control of any Excluded Driver. I understand that this exclusion will apply to all future renewals, reinstatements, and changes in the policy unless I notify the company in writing that I wish to revoke the Exclusion of any driver and pay any additional premium.
