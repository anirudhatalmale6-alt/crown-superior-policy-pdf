The State of Georgia requires that Uninsured Motorist Coverage must be offered at limits equal to the Bodily Injury and Property Damage Liability Limits of your policy, unless you reject this coverage or chose lower limits of coverage. You have the legal right to purchase Uninsured Motorist Coverage, and we recommend that you purchase this coverage. Uninsured Motorist Coverage protects the named insured, their resident relatives, and any passengers occupying the insured vehicle if they sustain bodily injury or property damage in an accident for which the owner or operator of the vehicle is legally liable and does not have insurance (uninsured) or does not have enough insurance (underinsured).

You may choose to purchase Uninsured Motorist Coverage — Added On To At-Fault Liability Limits. Under the “Added On To At-Fault Liability Limits” option, the entire limit of Uninsured Motorist Coverage is available in addition to any amounts payable under the liability coverage, if any, of the at-fault driver.

If you reject the Uninsured Motorist Coverage — Added On To At-Fault Liability Limits, you may select Uninsured Motorist Coverage — Reduced By At-Fault Liability. Under the Uninsured Motorist Coverage — Reduced By At-Fault Liability Limits option, the limit of coverage you select may be reduced by any amount payable under the liability coverage of the at-fault driver. This means that the coverage will pay only up to the difference between the at-fault driver’s liability coverage and the limit you purchased for your Uninsured Motorist — Reduced by At-Fault Liability Limits Coverage. This option is available at a reduced premium.
